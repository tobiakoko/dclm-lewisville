# Staff training: 30 minutes

For the person, or two people, who will keep the website up to date. The trainer brings a laptop, and the trainees each have a Sanity login. Practise on the **staging** dataset, not the live site.

| Time | Topic | Trainee does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 min | **What the site is for.** First-time visitors on phones deciding whether to come on Sunday. Members come second. | Opens the live site on their phone and finds the service time, address and "Plan your visit" |
| 5–10 min | **The Studio.** Signing in at `/studio`; editable pages compared with lists; draft compared with **Publish**; changes appear within a minute | Signs in and opens **Church details** |
| 10–15 min | **Changing a service time** ([runbook](RUNBOOK.md#change-a-service-time)), including a one-off schedule change | Changes Friday's end time, publishes, sees it on the home page and in the footer, then changes it back |
| 15–20 min | **Adding a special event** | Adds a practice event, sees it on Events, deletes it |
| 20–25 min | **Rules that protect the church.** Verbatim text (doctrines, Pastor's Welcome, two bios) is changed only with the pastor's approval; never guess facts; photos are real and described truthfully; testimonies only with consent | Explains in their own words what they'd do if a Zoom link isn't ready yet (answer: leave it empty) |
| 25–30 min | **Giving details, sermons, testimonies, and who to call** | Reads the runbook sections for these; notes the developer's contact |

After the session: both trainees can do the service-time and event tasks without help. Give them [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md).
