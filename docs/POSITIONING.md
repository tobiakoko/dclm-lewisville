# Positioning

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)
- **Character**: someone in Lewisville looking for a church home
- **Desire (ONE thing)**: to belong — to be known, not just counted
- **Problem — external**: no church home yet; doesn't know where to worship
- **Problem — internal**: fit-in anxiety — "I'm not sure I'd fit in" (decision 2026-08-14)
- **Problem — philosophical**: no one should feel lost in the crowd — especially inside a church
- **Villain**: being lost in the crowd / anonymity
- **Guide**: DCLM Lewisville — empathy ("we know what it's like to wonder if you'll fit in") + authority (worldwide Deeper Life Bible Church family founded by Pastor (Dr.) W.F. Kumuyi; regional overseer; local pastor who knows members personally)
- **Plan (Process)**: 1) Tell us you're coming (Plan Your Visit). 2) Visit Sunday 9:30 AM or Tuesday 7 PM. 3) Meet us — we'll know your name.
- **CTA**: Direct = "Plan Your Visit"; Transitional = "New here? Read what we believe" → /about
- **Failure stakes**: another Sunday spent alone, still searching for a church home
- **Success stakes**: walk in and be known by name — a family that grows with you in faith

## One-Liner
"We help people in Lewisville find a church home where they're known by name — not lost in the crowd." (chosen 2026-08-14, cocktail-party test pass)
